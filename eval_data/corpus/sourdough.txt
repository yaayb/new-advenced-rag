Sourdough Bread Fermentation
Sourdough bread relies on a starter, a culture of wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria. A healthy starter doubles in volume within four to eight hours of feeding at room temperature. Bulk fermentation usually lasts five to six hours at 24 degrees Celsius. Cold retarding the shaped dough in a refrigerator overnight deepens flavour and makes scoring easier. Baking in a preheated Dutch oven traps steam and produces a crisp crust.
