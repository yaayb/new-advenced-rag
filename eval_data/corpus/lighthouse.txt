The Calder Point Lighthouse
The Calder Point Lighthouse was completed in 1874 on a granite headland in the north of the island. Its tower rises 38 metres and its lamp was originally fuelled by whale oil before conversion to kerosene in 1902. The keeper's cottage housed up to three families during winter storms. Automation in 1987 ended the need for resident keepers, and the site now operates as a small maritime museum.
